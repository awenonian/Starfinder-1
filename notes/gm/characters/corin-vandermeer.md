# Corin Vandermeer

Human, forties. Client of record on the Vandermeer job — retained Eyeswide to
document that his mother is being financially exploited by her live-in carer.
Runs **Vandermeer Fittings**, a supply unit on Ring Three Level Nine: fasteners,
seals, gaskets, coupling hardware, sorted into bins by thread pitch by somebody
who cared once. Dust on the bins. One of three overhead strips dead.

## VOICE

Verbatim, session 2, at the shop:

- "Sorry — sorry, I thought you were — no. Come in. Sorry about the — we're between deliveries."
- "She's seventy-one and she is *sharp*, I want to be clear about that, she's sharper than I am."
- "My mother worked forty years in the Cantrell yards and she is *eating* on thirty-four credits a month, and Varn is sat in her spare room on a salary I pay."
- "That's not a person who snapped, that's a person who *waited.*"
- "When she's angry she doesn't shout, she just gets *polite.*"
- "Fine. So maybe it's longer. That's *worse*, isn't it?"

Over-talks when nervous, which is most of the time. Apologises for the shop.
Rehearses sentences and then delivers them slightly too fast. Says numbers like
a man who has said them to himself many times alone.

**Can't say:** that he wants the money. He has never articulated it and would be
genuinely horrified to hear it said. Also cannot finish a sum that leads
anywhere near his own benefit — watch him start "that's more than two thousand
credits—" and stop.

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the client who aimed the investigator with a true statement
  that wasn't the whole thing. A Oash in miniature, arriving before the player
  can recognise the shape. **Do not point this out.**
- **What he wants:** stated — his mother protected and a document a court will
  accept. Actual — control of her finances, which means the pension and, though
  he has never let himself think the word, **the flat, which she owns outright.**
- **What he'd never do:** confront Odett himself, or ask his mother for anything
  directly. He is forty-five and frightened of his mother being *polite* at him.
- **He is not lying and is not a villain.** He sincerely believes what he says.
  He believes it because *my mother is being defrauded* is a bearable thought
  where *I want her money* is not.

## POSITIONS

Retained Eyeswide against his mother's carer, without telling his mother, using
a read-only credential she gave him for a different purpose. That is a position
and he took it eleven days ago.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** the shape of the transfers as far as his portal shows him. That he
  pays Odett's salary. That his mother keeps every document she has ever
  touched.
- **Believes:** that the payments started eighteen months ago, because that is
  where the statement starts. Wrong, and he found out it was wrong in front of
  Shanhi and did not follow it anywhere.
- **Lies about:** the cash box. If asked about the inspector he will say it was
  a supplier and it will be the first outright untruth he has told her.
- **Won't say:** what Merrit Kav has on him. See below.

## The secret — resolved on establishment, session 2

**Corin has been buying salvage-grade fasteners and reselling them as new** for
about four years. It is fraud, it is ongoing, and it is the only reason the shop
is still solvent.

**Merrit Kav**, goods inspector at **Cantrell Yard 6** — one of Corin's
customers — found the substitution five weeks ago on a routine spot check. He
did not report it. He came back a week later with a number.

- **150 credits a month, in cash.** First payment three weeks ago. Second
  payment handed over **in front of Shanhi**, 1043, session 2 — a cash box, no
  goods, no chit, no receipt, and a man who pocketed it without counting.
- **Corin retained Eyeswide eleven days ago** — between the first payment and
  the second. That is why now.

**Kav wants** to not work another twenty years. He has done this once before to
a different supplier and it worked, which is how he knows it works. **He would
never** escalate to violence; he is a paperwork man and this is a paperwork
crime. **He does not know Talia Vandermeer exists** and would not care.

**None of this connects to Meridian, Kray, Ring Seven, or the old case.** It is
a small ugly thing in a small ugly shop. It is also the reason a guardianship
petition is worth 150 credits a month to Corin, and the reason the flat matters
more than the pension.

**The mirror, which is the point:** Corin is doing to a customer exactly what he
accuses Odett of doing to his mother, and the money he is chasing is money he
would hand straight to the man squeezing him. Do not have anyone say this.
