# Huvv

Skittermander. Runs a noodle counter on a mid-level concourse. Hired Shanhi for 40
credits to catch a supplier who turned out to be innocent, and got handed proof
that her night manager of two years has been robbing her instead.

## VOICE

- "You're here! You're here *early*. Is that — is it good? Is it a good early or a bad early?"
- "He's been apologising to me. He's been apologising to *me*, and I let him."
- "You broke my—" *(looks at it)* "It's a lock."
- "I'm going to ask him."
- "I can't give you any more money — I'm sorry, I really — I haven't got it. But I've got noodles."

Six arms and no interior life whatsoever: she has her thoughts **out loud**,
including the ones she hasn't finished. Sentences run out mid-clause when she
arrives somewhere she didn't expect. Bright and loud with customers, and it
switches on and off like a light.

**Can't say:** anything privately. She is incapable of a considered pause — if
she's working something out, you're watching her do it. She also can't perform
composure; when she's upset she puts two hands in her apron and lets the other
four carry on.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the small honest world Shanhi works in, and the proof that
  people in it make expensive, decent choices without being able to afford them.
- **What she wants:** to not be robbed, and — more — to not have been *stupid*.
  Now: for Teren to have a reason.
- **What she'd never do:** call Station Security on someone before she's spoken
  to them. She said why, out loud, unprompted: *"if I go to Station Security
  this morning then what I did was, I found out a man was drowning and I called
  the police on him. That's what that is."*

## POSITIONS

- **Chose to ask Teren herself** rather than report or dismiss him. In front of
  Shanhi, who did not argue. Cost her surprise and any chance of recovering stock.
- **Fed Shanhi** after being told Shanhi broke into her premises. Did not react to
  the lock at all.
- **Asked Shanhi to come back.** Not for work. Unanswered.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** her own numbers, badly. Her suppliers. Two years of Teren.
- **Believes** *(and was wrong)*: that **Rennick**, her supplier, was shorting
  her. She was short with him on the comm for six weeks while he apologised and
  knocked eight credits off because he felt bad. She now has to go and fix that.
- **Lies about:** nothing. She is constitutionally unable.
- **Won't say:** why she couldn't do the nights when she opened. Started to, then
  said "it doesn't matter." **Resolved: a long illness that took most of a year
  and most of her savings. She is fine now and does not discuss it.** (Player
  does not know.)

## Facts

- Leaves the float in the till overnight — about 80 credits — because the
  morning rush starts before she can reach a machine. It has been correct every
  morning for six weeks. Teren has walked past it two hundred times.
- Bad at numbers, knows it, asked Teren to do the books on Fourth-days for
  exactly that reason.
