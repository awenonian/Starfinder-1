# Odett Varn

Human, forties. Licensed live-in carer, three years in post with **Talia
Vandermeer** at 6-114. Tea-towel over one shoulder, sleeves pushed up, opens a
door about a third of the way and keeps a hand on the edge of it.

**Corin Vandermeer pays her salary.** She is loyal to Talia. Both of those are
load-bearing.

## VOICE

Verbatim, session 2, on the doorstep:

- "Yes?"
- "Which agency would that be." *(and then, unchanged in tone, again)*
- "I'm not being rude. But I don't let people in about her money. Not the bank, not the terrace, not anybody."
- "If there's a discrepancy you can put it in writing to her and she'll read it, because she reads everything."
- "Nobody, love. Terrace association."

Flat, unhurried, entirely unembarrassed. Repeats a question in exactly the same
tone rather than escalating, and will not get tired before you do.

**Can't say:** where the money goes. Talia asked her not to, once, three years
ago, and that was the end of the discussion as far as Odett is concerned.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the one person in this campaign who **lies**, and lies
  well, and is lying out of loyalty rather than guilt. Shanhi has **Lie to Me**.
  Let the feat catch her. What it catches is not theft.
- **What she wants:** to keep her licence and keep her word, in that order only
  because losing the first loses her the second.
- **What she'd never do:** tell Corin anything, or let a stranger past the door
  on the subject of Talia's finances. She'd also never take a credit off Talia;
  she is exactly as clean as her licence says.

## POSITIONS

**Session 2, the doorstep.** Lied to Talia's face about who was at the door —
*"Nobody, love. Terrace association."* — with Shanhi standing there watching her
do it. Cost: Talia caught it immediately and said so out loud, and Odett is now
a woman who has been caught lying to the person she looks after, in front of a
witness, over something she still won't explain.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** that Talia sends the sixty credits herself, every month, and
  roughly why. You cannot live in a house and not know.
- **Believes:** that keeping it is the right thing, and that Corin is circling.
  Both correct.
- **Lies about:** the caller, the subject, and anything adjacent to the money.
  Competently, flatly, and about the wrong thing.
- **Won't say:** Oduya. She may not know the name — she knows there is a debt of
  conscience and has never asked for the details, which is its own kind of
  loyalty.

## The tell she already gave

When Shanhi said *"a discrepancy in your financials,"* Odett did not look puzzled.
No half-second of *what discrepancy.* What crossed her face was **recognition**,
suppressed immediately. **Delivered, session 2.** She knows exactly which sixty
credits are in question — and a player will read that as guilt, which is the
point and is honest, because the truth is over-supported elsewhere.
