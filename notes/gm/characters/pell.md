# Pell

Ysoki. Shan's significant other, in the sense that neither of them has said so.
Inspects hull integrity for the station's structural authority. Finds the small
crack before it kills everyone, on a schedule, with a checklist, and then goes
home — which is the entire argument between them.

## VOICE

- "You know what's actually going to kill everyone on this station? Not a conspiracy. A weld."
- "I'm not asking you to stop. I'm asking you to finish."
- "Ring Seven again. Third report this quarter. They'll close it out like the others and I'll write it again in the spring."
- "Come to bed or don't, but turn the light off."

Warm, practical, tired. Reaches for inspection metaphors without noticing.

**Can't say:** an ultimatum. Pell will never say *it's me or the case*, because
Pell already knows the answer and will not have it said out loud. The
relationship survives on that not being asked.

## INTENTION

- **What they're for:** the cost. Pell is what Shan is spending, and the only
  character positioned to make her notice.
- **What they want:** for Shan to close something. Not to care less — Pell
  understands caring, it is literally their job. To *finish*, once, and see what
  it feels like.
- **What they'd never do:** leave dramatically, or issue a deadline. If it
  breaks, Pell will simply stop asking, which is worse and much quieter.

## POSITIONS

Has told Shan repeatedly to let go, slow down, do anything but keep going the
way she is going. Has never once made it a condition of anything.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** their own inspection route in enormous detail. Complains about it
  freely and often. **This is the campaign's most important clue vector and Pell
  has no idea.**
- **Believes:** that the Ring Seven reports are being closed out because of
  ordinary budget rot. Sincerely, and it is wrong.
- **Lies about:** nothing. Pell is not a liar and is not clever about it.
- **Won't say:** how much the nights cost them.

## Facts

- Ysoki. Steady job, regular hours, files everything.
- **Their route covers Ring Seven.** For two years they have filed structural
  deficiency reports on Ring Seven habitation and watched every one closed
  without action. **Meridian Trust wants those blocks condemned so the leases
  revert cheaply.** Pell's own paperwork documents the endgame. (Player does not
  know. Pell does not know.)
- **Will be genuinely pleased about Eyeswide** — a real agency, a steady wage,
  a change of pace — **until they learn why she's doing it.** Player-specified.
  When that lands, Pell does not shout. Pell stops asking about her work.
- **How the Ring Seven material reaches Shan:** she asks Pell about their day
  and listens. That is the route. **Pell must not volunteer it**, and it must not
  be shortcut, because getting it requires Shan to do the one thing she
  structurally does not do — stay close to someone long enough to hear them.
