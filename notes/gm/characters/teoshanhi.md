# Teoshanhi — "Shan"

The player character. A pahtra ex-detective who was thrown off the force for
not dropping a case, and who is trying to get inside the Eyeswide Agency
because their resources might finally get her the evidence she couldn't get
alone. Sheet: `notes/player-owned/teoshanhi.md`.

## VOICE

Not yet established — she hasn't spoken in play. Fill this in from the first
session, verbatim, and don't invent samples before then.

What is already known about the shape of it, from session zero: **she gives
people facts, not arguments.** Her intervention in the diagnostic was three
words on a piece of paper — "he's lying" — with no explanation attached and no
attempt to steer what the woman did with it. Expect terseness where another
character would justify.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the campaign's engine. She is a person who cannot leave a
  thread alone, pointed at an institution that punished her for it once already.
  Every situation should offer her a thread.

- **What she wants:** the evidence. Three years ago she worked out what was
  actually happening and could not prove any of it, and was removed from the
  force for continuing. She wants into the Eyeswide Agency because their
  resources and their psychic training might get her what she couldn't get on
  her own. She is not looking for vindication in front of anyone — see below.

- **How she goes about it:** relentlessly and *indirectly*. She gathers before
  she moves. She acts from outside the situation where she can. Given a choice
  between confronting someone and slipping past them, she slips past.

- **What moves her:** somebody being lied to who doesn't know it, in a way
  that's going to cost them. That is the specific trigger — not injustice in
  the abstract, not cruelty, not danger. Being *deceived*, with a price
  attached. Put that in front of her and she cannot walk away.

- **Her threshold.** She is not a crusader about every lie. In the diagnostic
  she was willing to let the man's story pass if he simply left — she waits to
  see whether it is going to cost anyone. This matters: **give her lies that
  don't matter and she'll let them go.** The hook is the cost, not the lie.

- **What she'd never do:**
  - **Stay to be thanked.** She warns people and leaves before it lands. She
    will not attach herself to the person she helped, and she does not manage
    what they do with what she gave them.
  - **Manage someone's decision for them.** She hands over the fact and lets
    them choose, even when she can see they'll choose badly.
  - **Drop it once she's decided it matters.** This is the thing that cost her
    her career and she has not learned from it and will not.

- **The tension to play on.** "Leaves no stone unturned" and "will not attach
  herself to anyone" are the two halves of her, and they pull against each
  other. A case she cannot work from the outside — one that requires her to be
  *in* it, known, owed to, relied upon — is where she is most interesting and
  most uncomfortable. Build at least one.

## POSITIONS

None yet — nothing has happened in play.

Her one historical position, which she took before the campaign starts and has
never withdrawn: **she was told to stop working the case and she kept working
it.** She lost her job over it and she has not changed her mind.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** what she worked out three years ago about the old case. See
  `notes/gm/STATE.md` for what that is and, more importantly, for how much of
  the truth it actually covers.
- **Believes:** that she got to the bottom of it. She got part of the way.
- **Won't say:** she is not forthcoming about why she left the force. The chip
  on her shoulder is real and she does not display it.

## Facts

- Pahtra, from Pulonis originally or descended from it — **open, ask the player
  whether she was born on Absalom Station.** Pahtra culture is competitive,
  musical, tradition-heavy; her people's homeworld was under Veskarium
  occupation within living memory and joined the Pact Worlds recently.
- Detective with Absalom Station's police for long enough to be good at it.
  Dismissed roughly three years ago.
- Private investigator since. Making ends meet, not thriving.
- Speaks Common, Pahtra, Vesk, Castrovelian, Trinary.
- Darkvision. Works in the dark by preference and by build.
- **Others probably assume she's bad with computers** — a common prejudice about
  pahtras, per her ancestry. She is not trained in Computers, so in her case
  it happens to be true, which is worse.
