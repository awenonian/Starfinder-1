# Teren

Human. Huvv's night manager for two years. Has been selling about a fifth of
each delivery out the service door on the nights he closes, for six weeks, to
service a debt he cannot pay.

Has not appeared in a scene yet — Shanhi has only watched him. **No verbatim
lines exist.** What follows is intended voice, not recorded voice; replace it
with real samples the moment he speaks.

## VOICE (intended, not yet spoken)

Quiet, tidy, apologetic about small things and not about large ones. The
register of a man who has decided what he is doing is survivable and does not
want it examined.

**Can't say:** that he is owed anything, or that it wasn't his fault. He isn't
looking for absolution and would find being forgiven harder than being fired.

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the first small proof that the people Shanhi catches are
  people. And a rhyme, in miniature, with the campaign's real subject: debt on
  the lower levels, and what it makes ordinary people do.
- **What he wants:** to clear **940 credits** before the interest resets.
  **Next payment due nine days from session 1.**
- **What he'd never do:** take from the till. It is unlocked overnight with
  about 80 credits in it and he has walked past it two hundred times. He
  considers stock and cash to be different things, and that distinction is what
  lets him sleep.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** exactly what he has taken and when. Who buys it.
- **Believes:** that he can get out from under this before anyone notices.
- **Lies about:** he has never had to. Nobody has asked him anything.

## Facts — including what happened offscreen

- **The debt is to a small predatory lender** operating from a debt-relief
  storefront two levels down, the sort with a hand-lettered sign about *dignity*
  and *a fresh start*. **It has NO connection to Anselm Kray or Meridian Trust.**
  This is deliberate. It rhymes with the real conspiracy and connects to nothing.
  **Do not retroactively wire it up to reward a good guess.**
- Aborted the fifth and sixth crates on the night of session 1 after hearing the
  broken motion plate. Walked home the long way without looking back.
