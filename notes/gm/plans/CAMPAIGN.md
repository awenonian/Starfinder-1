# Campaign

Filled in at the close of session zero, so that no later session has to ask
again.

- **System:** Starfinder Second Edition
- **Started from:** character creation, 1st level
- **Rules skill in use:** `starfinder-player-core` and `starfinder-gm-core`.
  **Check both before stating a rule.** Prior knowledge of this system is
  unreliable.
- **House rules and table conventions:**
  - **Free Archetype variant** (GM Core) is on: an extra class feat at 2nd level
    and every even level after, usable only for archetype feats. Agreed as
    compensation for solo play. Revisit if a second character joins.
  - **Solo encounter budgets.** One PC means three missing characters. A single
    on-level enemy is an *Extreme* encounter. Full table in `notes/gm/STATE.md`
    — read it before building any fight.
  - **The audible inner monologue.** Standing ruling in `notes/gm/STATE.md`.
  - **Out-of-book content.** The Eyeswide Detective archetype (Starfinder
    Absalom Station p.132) is not in the bundled skills, and both Archives of
    Nethys and Demiplane are blocked by the session's network proxy. **Ask the
    player to paste any archetype text needed.** The dedication is recorded on
    the character sheet.

## Lines and veils

**Settled. The GM does not ask about this at the start of a session.** Lines
stay out entirely; veils can exist but happen off-page.

- **Lines:** **violence to children**; **body horror**
- **Veils:** none named

The player added: "Everything else should be fine, but I'll speak up if that
doesn't stay true." That is standing permission that can be withdrawn at any
moment, not a blanket clearance.

The live safety mechanisms don't depend on this: the player can stop or rewind
anything at any moment, and the GM checks in before and after a scene that goes
somewhere heavy.

## Table commands

- **"stop" or "skip that"** — said out of character at any point. Obeyed
  immediately, no questions asked. To undo something that already happened, use
  the client's own rollback.
- **`log: <anything>`** — appended verbatim to `playtests/current.md` and play
  carries on.

## The long shape

**The character.** Teoshanhi — "Shan" — a pahtra operative, born on Absalom
Station. Was a police detective and a good one. Was told to stop working a case,
didn't, was right, couldn't prove it, and was thrown off the force for it. Has
been a private eye since. Sheet: `notes/player-owned/teoshanhi.md`.

**What the player wants to be doing**, close to verbatim:

> "I think investigation primarily, which might entail a lot of things. There
> may also be a change later, say, if Shanhi realizes that providing proof will
> not provide justice."

> "Combat is fine, Starfinder is built around it, but unless I get other party
> members, the challenge will have to be calibrated."

> "Tone, I think I want it a little dark, but with a funny tone on it. Think the
> Calvin and Hobbes strips where he's pretending to be a detective. I think it
> would be fun/funny to do a classic noir inner monologue, but I'll be
> telepathic, so other people can hear it. The characters play it straight, even
> if it's comedic."

**On tone specifically.** The comedy is in the register, not the events. Calvin
playing detective is funny because he is completely sincere and the world is
completely ordinary. **The characters play it straight — nobody in the fiction
winks.** The humour lives in the gap between Shan's narration and what is
actually happening, and it dies the moment an NPC finds her funny.

**On the pahtra material.** The player was clear: Absalom-born, no immigrant
angle, "I'm not really suited for it." Pahtra culture is **flavour, not thread**
— available as texture and as the reason a vesk and a pahtra started off wary of
each other. The player did explicitly sanction one use: *"if you want an NPC that
criticizes me for not achieving enough, that's fine."* That NPC is Ifset. Do not
build a homeland arc.

**Act one is an investigation** pointed at the Eyeswide Agency and at the case
that cost her the job.

**Act two is planned and agreed.** The player's own framing: if Shan works out
that providing proof will not provide justice, the campaign changes into
something else. The structure has been built so that question has a **real
answer waiting rather than a twist** — see `notes/gm/STATE.md`. The player's
stated intention:

> "yes, it's fine if it changes then. I will consider it an actual act 2, so
> we'll probably to another session 0 then (the system is designed to handle
> that, just by recreating the session zero document)"

**So: when act one resolves, recreate `notes/gm/plans/SESSION-ZERO.md`** with
the boilerplate in `.claude/rules/session-zero.md` § Re-arming it. That is the
agreed plan, not a suggestion — do not improvise act two out of the tail of
act one.
