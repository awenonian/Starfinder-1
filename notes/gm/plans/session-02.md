**Written at the close of session 1. Nothing here has happened.**

Read `notes/player-facing/RECORD.md` (session 1), `notes/gm/STATE.md` (the

## Where we left off

Shan is standing at Huvv's counter at about 0910, having been awake for over
twenty-four hours, having just been fed by a woman she burgled, with an
unanswered invitation hanging in the air. **The Eyeswide meeting is 0900, two
days out, Ring Three office.**

## What this session probably has to do

1. **The Eyeswide meeting.** Oash, in person. This is the session's centre of
   gravity and the player has been pointed at it since session zero.
2. **The dedication lands at 2nd level** — and with it the limited telepathy,
   and with it the leaking monologue. **Do not trail this in advance.** Let the
   first leak happen and let the NPC respond to the content, flat, as though
   she'd spoken. See the ruling in `notes/gm/STATE.md` and its three bounds.
3. **Give Oash the assignment.** Something small and near, chosen. Per Oash's
   file: *"Take the Vandermeer thing. It's small and it's near, and I'd like to
   see how you move."* That name is unresolved — **resolve it before using it.**

## Two days to fill, or to skip

Cut to the meeting if the player wants. But the intervening days hold real
things, all of them optional and none of them urgent:

- **Pell.** Shan is about to take an agency job. Pell will be genuinely pleased
  — until they learn why. That conversation is available now and it is the
  campaign's best pressure point. **Do not have Pell volunteer the Ring Seven
  material.** It comes from Shan asking about their day and listening.
- **Huvv's invitation.** Answering it, or not, or going back and finding out
  what happened with Teren.
- **Sleep.** She genuinely needs it and hasn't had any.

## Do not

- **Do not connect Teren's lender to Kray or Meridian.** It rhymes deliberately
  and connects to nothing. If the player assumes a link, the world simply does
  not confirm it — no nudge in either direction.
- **Do not confirm or deny any theory the player forms**, including by warming
  up when they're right or going quiet when they're wrong.
- **Do not have Oash lie.** Every evasion she makes is a true statement. That is
  the constraint the whole character runs on and it is what makes her catchable
  in retrospect.

on-level enemy is an Extreme encounter.** Recalculate on level-up. Hero Point at
