# The Vandermeer thing — RESOLVED

*Resolved at the top of session 2, per the instruction above. Nothing here has
reached play yet. This is Oash's assignment: small, near, on Ring Three.*

**The case as it is presented.** **Corin Vandermeer**, human, mid-forties, has
retained Eyeswide to document that his mother is being financially exploited by
her live-in carer. He wants it in writing, with dates and amounts, suitable for
a guardianship petition.

- **Talia Vandermeer**, human, 71. Retired ship-fitter, forty years in the
  Ring Three fitting yards. Lives alone on a residential terrace with a licensed
  live-in carer. Sharp. Slow on her feet, not in her head.
- **Odett Varn**, human, forties, licensed professional carer. Three years with
  Talia.
- **What is verifiably true:** money leaves Talia's account every month.
  Consistently, for a long time, in an amount that matters. Corin has the
  statements and they say exactly what he says they say.

**The truth.** Talia is sending the money herself, and has been for
thirty-eight years. A scaffold gave way in the fitting yards and a man named
**Bevin Oduya** died. The rigging call was Talia's. The finding was
material fatigue and nobody was disciplined. She has paid a monthly sum to
Oduya's daughter ever since, through an anonymous transfer clerk, and has never
told the daughter, or her son, or anyone but Odett.

- **Odett knows,** because you cannot live in a house and not know. Talia asked
  her not to say. **Odett will lie to Shanhi to keep that** — flatly, competently,
  and about the wrong thing. She is one of the very few people in this campaign
  who lies at all, and Shanhi has **Lie to Me**. Let the feat catch her. What it
  catches is loyalty, not theft.
- **Talia will not explain herself.** Not out of shame — she settled this with
  herself decades ago and considers it nobody's business. Asked directly and
  respectfully, she says a true thing that is not the answer: *"It's my money and
  it goes where I send it."*
- **The daughter is real**: **Nera Oduya**, works freight scheduling two rings
  over, has assumed for years the payments are some kind of pension
  administration error she'd be a fool to query.

**Corin is not a monster and is not lying.** He sincerely believes his mother is
being robbed. He believes it because his own fitting-supply business has been
failing for two years, he has two children, and *my mother is being defrauded*
is a bearable thought where *I want her money* is not. He has never articulated
the second one and would be genuinely horrified by it.

**Why this is the assignment.** Oash wants to watch how Shanhi moves, and this
case has no version where the paperwork answers it:

- Shanhi's method is to hand over the fact and refuse to advocate. Here the fact —
  *the money is leaving, on schedule, in that amount* — is **true, complete, and
  hands a woman's autonomy to her son.** The person who gets to choose is not the
  person it happens to.
- It rhymes with Oash on purpose: **a client aimed her with a true statement
  that was not the whole thing.** Do not point this out. It is available later,
  in retrospect, exactly like Oash's own dialogue.
- **It is on Ring Three.** Upstairs, domestic, and nowhere near the lower
  levels. That is the first data point for CLUE D2 — Oash steers her assignments
  away from one specific direction — and it must not be flagged as such.

**Oash does not know the truth of it.** It is a real case that came in the door.
She chose it because it is small, near, and observable.

**Do not:** connect Oduya, Corin's debts, or Odett to Meridian, Kray, or the
Ring Seven leases. Nothing here connects to anything. It is a real case in a
real city.
