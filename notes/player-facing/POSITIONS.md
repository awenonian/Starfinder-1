# Positions

Something a character committed to, out loud or by acting: what they said, to
whom, and what it cost them.

A man who looks at stolen property in a cart and decides to say nothing has
taken a position, and it is the most important thing that happened in that
scene.

**After every scene, ask: did anyone here take a position?**

Positions taken don't survive unless they're written down. A remembered one is
already lost.

Format, loosely:

> **Who** — what they committed to. To whom. What it cost. *(session/scene)*

---

> **Teoshanhi** — told Huvv the whole truth, unprompted and against her own
> interest: that she broke the lock trying to be thorough, and that she went
> through Teren's coat. Gave the findings first and the apology second, and
> named Teren's debt as motive without recommending anything. Cost her the
> clean version of her own conduct and any leverage she had over how it looked.
> *(Session 1, the concourse, 0834)*

> **Huvv** — having been handed proof that her night manager of two years has
> been robbing her for six weeks, committed out loud to **asking him herself**
> rather than reporting him or dismissing him first. Said in front of Shan, who
> did not talk her out of it. Costs her the element of surprise and any chance
> of recovering the stock. *(Session 1, the concourse, 0834)*

> **Vey Torrance** — in a bar full of retired Station Security, out loud, said
> to Shan's face that the department had gone easy on her: no charge sheet, no
> conduct finding, no licence hold. Volunteered it unasked, drew no conclusion
> from it, and moved on. Cost her a little standing in the only room she has
> left — the man two stools down was listening, and said so.
> *(Session 2, the Loading Gate, 2140)*
