# Player-facing

What the player knows, or what people in the world know. Uncertainty is fine
here — this is the shared picture, and the shared picture is allowed to be
wrong. What's actually true lives in `notes/gm/`.

- `RECORD.md` — what happened, in play order. Appended as you go, never tidied.
  Quote the player rather than describing them.
- `STATE.md` — the ledger. Terse, ugly, revised freely.
- `POSITIONS.md` — what characters committed to, out loud or by acting.
