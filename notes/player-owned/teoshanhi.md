## Class features

**Sharpshooter — Aim** (◆, Concentrate, Operative): designate one creature
within your first range increment as your **mark**. Your ranged Strikes against
your mark deal **+1d4 precision damage** and reduce its cover bonus by 1. One
mark at a time.

**Ghost specialization exploit:** you can **Aim at a hidden or undetected target
as a free action.** Pairs with darkvision — she is at her best working in the
dark against people who don't know where she is.

## Feats

- **Ancestry — Harmonic Sensitivity:** trained in Performance (or Assurance
  (Performance) if already trained). Can accurately recall any melody or lyrics
  heard within the last week; **+2 circumstance bonus to Deception and
  Performance checks involving mimicking speech or a specific sound.**
- **Operative — Mobile Aim** (Operative, Traversal): Stride and then Aim with a
  gun you're wielding. *(OPEN — see alternatives in session notes.)*
- **Skill (Ghost) — Lie to Me:** use Deception to detect lies.
- **Skill (background) — Streetwise:** use your Society modifier to Gather
  Information; in your home settlement, use Society to Recall Knowledge
  (higher DC, no time cost).
